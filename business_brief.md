# Stakeholder Request — Head of Operations

**To:** Data Analyst, Last-Mile Operations  
**From:** Head of Operations  
**Subject:** Investigation of declining on-time delivery performance  
**Reporting period:** 1 January–31 December 2025

Over the last two quarters, operations managers have raised concerns about late deliveries. Some believe we need more drivers. Others believe the issue is concentrated in a small number of hubs or evening periods. We do not yet have a consistent explanation.

I need an investigation that distinguishes between:
- time spent preparing an order;
- time waiting for a driver to be assigned;
- time between assignment and pickup;
- time spent travelling to the customer.

Please do not assume that all late orders have the same cause. Compare hubs and zones fairly, taking their different SLA targets and delivery profiles into account.

The immediate decision is whether we should:
1. recruit additional drivers;
2. redistribute existing shifts across hubs and peak periods;
3. change zone-to-hub allocation;
4. address preparation or dispatch-process delays; or
5. run a more targeted operational test before committing resources.

Please provide a recommendation that includes the evidence, expected operational mechanism, risks, and a way to measure whether the change worked.

**Constraints**
- Do not recommend hiring based only on total order growth or a simple driver headcount.
- Do not compare hubs without considering differences in zone mix and SLA targets.
- Do not count cancelled orders as successful or late deliveries without a clearly stated metric rule.
- Separate descriptive association from causal claims.
- State data limitations and any assumptions that require confirmation by Operations.

**Deliverable**
A decision-focused Power BI report while explaining the problem, method, findings, limitations, and recommended next step.
