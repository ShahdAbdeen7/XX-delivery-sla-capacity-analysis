START HERE

1. Read README.md.
2. Read docs/business_brief.md as if it came from your Head of Operations.
3. Read docs/data_dictionary.md.
4. Do not clean the data yet.
5. Your first task is to inspect the raw files and write down:
   - the grain of each table;
   - candidate primary keys;
   - possible duplicate records;
   - missing / invalid timestamp patterns;
   - relationship questions;
   - business definitions you need clarified.

Do not build visuals or DAX in this first step. A good analyst reduces uncertainty before building a report.
